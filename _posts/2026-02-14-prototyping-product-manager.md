### The Shift

This isn't just a title — it's a required shift in Product Leadership. We must move beyond being a requirement gatherer and become a true builder.

### The Insight

The Product Managers who truly stand out are those who dedicate time to hacking a fresh idea and bringing a working prototype to the table in no time.

When a Product Manager demonstrates a prototype instead of merely explaining a PRD, they gain organizational influence — because a prototype showcases the "wow" (vision, speed, possibility) more effectively than documents ever could.

### Why Prototyping Wins

- **Vision becomes visible.** A prototype communicates intent faster than any slide deck.
- **Feedback becomes concrete.** Stakeholders can interact with the idea, not just imagine it.
- **Speed builds trust.** Showing a working demo in days instead of weeks signals competence and momentum.

### How to Start

1. Pick one feature or flow from your current roadmap.
2. Use browser-first tools (Canvas, Lovable, or even a simple HTML mockup) to build a rough version in a single afternoon.
3. Share it with one stakeholder and iterate based on their reaction.
4. Repeat until prototyping becomes your default communication method.

### The Bottom Line

In the AI era, there's no excuse for not having a prototype. The tools are fast, the barriers are low, and the impact is immediate. If you're still only writing PRDs, you're leaving influence on the table.
