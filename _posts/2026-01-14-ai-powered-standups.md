The daily standup. A ritual in every product and engineering team. You report what you did, explain your ETA, and raise issues for help.

But with AI, everything changes.

## The New Standup Conversation

**Person 1:**
> "I delegated the stories assigned to me to my agent. I'm now monitoring the changes deployed to pre-prod environment. I'll review the final status of the changes the agent made."

**Person 2:**
> "I found a gap from the previous week's implementation that the agent made. I'll now craft the instructions to fix it and update the test cases."

This isn't science fiction. This is happening now.

## What Changed?

Your role shifts from **doing** to **managing**. You're no longer the executor — you're the conductor.

- You assign tasks to agents
- You monitor their progress
- You review outputs
- You refine instructions when gaps appear

## The Real Shift

The standup isn't about reporting hours anymore. It's about:
- What agents you deployed
- What outputs you're reviewing
- Where you need to intervene

The question isn't "What did you do?" — it's "What did you delegate, review, and refine?"

Welcome to the future of work.
