The concept of power users is evolving, and if you're not paying attention, you'll miss the shift.

## The Old Power User

Gone are the days when power users just explored every feature and reported feedback. That model worked when software was static - you discovered hidden shortcuts, found edge cases, and sent bug reports.

## The New Power User

Now, power users are expected to leverage AI capabilities to achieve outcomes and explore new possibilities. Companies are not looking for users who just explore every inch of an app. They're looking for users who can explore AI features, provide feedback, and help shape the future of the product.

## What This Means in Practice

- **Outcome over exploration**: It's not about knowing every menu item. It's about using AI features to accomplish something that wasn't possible before.
- **Feedback that shapes the product**: When you use AI features deeply, your feedback becomes invaluable. You're not reporting a bug - you're reporting a gap in the AI's understanding or capability.
- **Advocacy through demonstration**: The best way to evangelize a product is to show what you built with it. Power users who share their AI-driven workflows become de facto ambassadors.

## How to Rebrand

- Learn the AI features of every tool you use - not just the basics, but the advanced capabilities.
- Build something non-trivial with them and share the result.
- Provide structured feedback that helps the product team improve the AI layer.
- Position yourself as the person on your team who knows how to get the most out of AI tools.

## The Bottom Line

The power user of 2026 isn't the person who knows every keyboard shortcut. They're the person who knows how to make AI do things the product team didn't anticipate. Rebrand accordingly.
