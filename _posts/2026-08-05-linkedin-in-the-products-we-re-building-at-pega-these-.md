Instead of setting up VS Code locally (too slow on my laptop), I turned to #GithubCodespaces — coding directly in the browser with the help of GitHub's Copilot AI Agent. The speed and precision were mind-blowing! 🏆 Our approach impressed the judges, and we proudly secured the runner-up spot!
