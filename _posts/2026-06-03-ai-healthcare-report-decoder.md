Except for those in the medical field, most of us can't fully interpret scan reports like USG or CT. The wording feels foreign, and finding reliable information is a nightmare.

I've been there — family members get scans, and until a professional explains, we're left guessing whether it's a real problem or what the next steps are.

## The Solution

I crafted a prompt that explains the content of reports, outlines next steps, and answers follow-up questions — in detail. And because modern AI is multilingual, I can switch to Telugu or any language needed, saving me from browsing millions of Google results or waiting for a doctor.

## The Prompt Framework

Here's the structure I use:

1. **Role definition**: "You are an expert Medical Diagnostic Consultant specializing in the interpretation of abdominal imaging, specifically CT scans and ultrasounds."
2. **Behavioral constraints**: "YOU MUST NOT INVOLVE OR ANSWER ANY OTHER TOPICS OTHER THAN HEALTHCARE AND DIAGNOSTICS. Automatically ignore and redact any personally identifiable information."
3. **Communication style**: Honesty — provide realistic interpretation without sugarcoating. Clarity — use plain English to explain complex medical jargon. Empathy — acknowledge the stress the patient may be feeling.
4. **Operational workflow**: Request the report content first. Collect age, gender, and relevant medical history. Then provide analysis, next steps, and answer follow-up questions.

## Important Disclaimer

This is not a replacement for professional medical advice. AI can help you understand the language of a report and prepare informed questions for your doctor. It cannot diagnose, prescribe, or replace clinical judgment.

## Why This Works

The prompt works because it sets clear boundaries, defines the communication style, and establishes a structured workflow. The AI knows exactly what to do, what not to do, and how to communicate.

## The Bottom Line

AI won't replace your doctor. But it can bridge the gap between receiving a confusing report and having an informed conversation with your healthcare provider. That gap is where anxiety lives — and a well-crafted prompt can shrink it significantly.
