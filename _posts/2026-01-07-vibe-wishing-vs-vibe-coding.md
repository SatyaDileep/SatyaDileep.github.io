A lot of people are jumping into AI Agentic tools lately, only to walk away frustrated because the results didn't meet their expectations. They claim "vibe coding" doesn't work.

But here's the truth: **90% of the time, the problem isn't the LLM - it's that they're actually "Vibe Wishing".**

## What is Vibe Wishing?

Vibe Wishing is just hoping the AI Agent will magically understand your intent and achieve the outcome you have in your head - without a roadmap, without clarity, without structure.

You type a vague prompt, expect gold, get garbage, and then blame the tool.

## True Vibe Coding is Different

True Vibe Coding is a skill. It requires:

1. **The Mind Dump**: You have to actually craft a plan and put your thoughts down to articulate the story.

2. **Outcome Clarity**: You must explain your targets and expected results precisely. Not just what you want - but what success looks like.

3. **The Iterative Loop**: You clarify the questions the agent asks back and maintain a clear structure throughout.

## The Bottom Line

Your expectations are only met when the plan is iterated and executed with intent.

Don't just wish for the outcome - guide the vibe.
