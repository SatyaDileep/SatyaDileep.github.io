AI tools are changing the game for how teams operate. Here's what I see happening in 2026 and beyond.

## Teams Will Shrink

Agile teams will achieve more with fewer people, driven by AI and automation. This is an uncomfortable truth — it may mean job cuts in roles that are heavily task-based and repetitive. But it also means the remaining team members will have more impact and more ownership.

## Roles Will Evolve

You'll need to understand other roles, empathize with users, and focus on product and UX. It's time to step up and own more than your narrow slice.

The engineer who understands product strategy will outperform the engineer who only writes code. The PM who understands technical constraints will outperform the PM who only writes requirements. Cross-functional fluency is the new competitive advantage.

## Productivity Will Leap

The opportunity is to leverage AI to focus on high-impact tasks, driving innovation and growth instead of spending time on the repetitive work that used to consume entire days.

## What This Means for You

- **Expand your scope**: Learn the basics of adjacent roles. Understand design, product, and business — not just your specialty.
- **Automate the boring**: Identify repetitive tasks in your workflow and build or adopt agents to handle them.
- **Focus on judgment**: AI can execute. Your value is in deciding what to execute and why.
- **Communicate better**: As teams shrink, each person's ability to articulate ideas, align stakeholders, and drive decisions becomes more critical.

## The Bottom Line

The future of work isn't about competing with AI. It's about leveraging it to do more meaningful work with fewer people. The teams that embrace this shift will outperform the ones that resist it.
