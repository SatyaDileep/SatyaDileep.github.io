#AI is drastically changing how we work, and the real competitive advantage now is Currently, I manage a personal "squad" of 8 to 10 #agents. I don’t use every single one every day, but they are essential for me to stay productive. Here is why: 🌟Defeating the "Boring": I identify repetitive, manual tasks—ex: creating a themed color palette or breaking down complex stories—and I build an agent to handle them. 🪄Constant Evolution: Management means staying ahead of the market. When #Gemini added Google Search integration, I didn't just "use" it—I upgraded my agents to ensure they were pulling real-time data instead of static info.
