### The Prediction

We are watching Tesla, Unitree Robotics, and Figure ship the first real wave of humanoid bots right now. By 2028, they'll be part of the furniture.

But here's the reality: **Hardware is messy.** It gets hot, it wears out, and it breaks.

### The Gap

Coding is becoming a commodity — AI is already building the brain. The real challenge moving forward is the body.

- Who swaps the 2027 sensors for 2030 tech?
- Who refurbishes the "pre-owned" bot market?
- Who handles the physical assembly that AGI can't reach?

### The Opportunity

AI can think, but it can't reach through a screen to fix a seized joint.

By 2030, the local "Computer Shop" will evolve into a Robotics Maintenance Hub. The people mastering the hardware — the ones who understand sensors, actuators, and mechanical wear — will be the most future-proof professionals in the AI economy.

### The Bottom Line

While the world builds amazing robots, there is a deep need for the ones who maintain them. If you're looking for a career pivot that AI can't automate, look at the physical layer. The brain is being built by machines. The body still needs humans.
