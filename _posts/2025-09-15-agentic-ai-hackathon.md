Participated in an Agentic AI hackathon with a focus on real fintech challenges, co‑building a seamless, gamified flow for Pine Labs payment gateway integration.

Started on a modest laptop with no local IDEs, led with product vision, then jumped into coding as ideas crystallized-prototyped with Lovable, switched to in‑browser development with GitHub Codespaces and Copilot to keep velocity high.

**Takeaway:** With browser‑based tooling and agent assistance, idea‑to‑demo can happen in hours-even on constrained hardware.
