### The Trap

We've all been there - feeling stranded or stuck in the daily grind. On the outside, it might look like you're just spinning your wheels. But internally, you're the only one who knows the value of the meaning you're building.

### The Insight

Every time I watch a talk by Guy Kawasaki, one idea stands out above all others: **"Making meaning is more important than making money."**

In a world obsessed with side hustles and scaling, that sounds like a bold claim. But when you apply it to your career, it's the ultimate truth.

### Why Meaning Wins

- **The deep satisfaction of solving a problem** - that moment when you know you've made something that matters.
- **The clarity of knowing exactly where you are headed** - even when the path isn't visible to others.
- **The future outcome that only you can see right now** - meaning is the lens that lets you endure the present for a better tomorrow.

### The Bottom Line

Meaning is the fuel. Money is just the result.

If you focus on the meaning, you can survive the times when you feel stranded - because you know the "why" behind your work. And that "why" is what keeps you building, iterating, and pushing forward when the easy path would be to quit.
