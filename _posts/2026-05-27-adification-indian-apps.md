Very soon, every app you use is going to be incredibly annoying. It's not because of bugs or mediocre performance. It's because of the forced ad-ification of the Indian app ecosystem.

## The New Standard Operating Procedure

The pattern is becoming universal:

1. Perform a successful action - order placed, booking confirmed, task completed.
2. Immediately get hit with an ad.
3. Relevant? Irrelevant? Doesn't matter. The platform just wants the impression.

The goal used to be seamless convenience. Now, the goal is simply ad real estate.

## Why This Is Happening

The economics are clear. User acquisition costs are rising, and platforms are under pressure to monetize every interaction. Ads are the easiest revenue lever to pull - easier than raising prices, easier than introducing premium tiers.

But the cost is user trust, and that's a harder metric to recover.

## The Product Lesson

There's a difference between monetization and exploitation. Monetization respects the user's intent and timing. Exploitation hijacks a moment of satisfaction and turns it into an interruption.

The platforms that win long-term will be the ones that find creative ways to monetize without degrading the core experience. The ones that treat every screen as ad inventory will lose users to the ones that don't.

## The Bottom Line

Convenience was the promise of the app economy. Ad-ification is the tax. The platforms that remember the promise will outlast the ones that only see the tax revenue.
